# Software client decision checklist

Use this before accepting a project. A concern is a question to investigate, not an automatic judgement about the client.

## Work and scope
- [ ] Can we describe the first useful outcome and the user?
- [ ] Are deliverables, exclusions and acceptance criteria clear enough to estimate?
- [ ] Have we identified dependencies and who can approve decisions?
- [ ] If important questions are unresolved, can we agree a discovery phase?

## Commercial and delivery fit
- [ ] Can the available budget cover a responsibly scoped first phase?
- [ ] Are payment milestones and terms agreed in writing before work starts?
- [ ] Is there enough team capacity for the date and existing commitments?
- [ ] Are the deadline and review times realistic? Are assumptions recorded?

## Safety and trust
- [ ] Do we have the skills and access to handle the data and security risks?
- [ ] Are ownership, authorised access and handover responsibilities clear?
- [ ] Is the requested use lawful and consistent with our standards?
- [ ] Can both sides communicate concerns and make decisions respectfully?

## Decision
- Proceed: record scope, conditions and next steps in writing.
- Re-scope: propose a specific smaller phase and confirm what is excluded.
- Decline: give a brief, honest reason. Refer someone only with their permission.

Do not accept until material unknowns are resolved to a level appropriate for the work. This checklist is not legal advice; seek qualified advice when needed.
