# MVP scoping call checklist

A first call produces a provisional outline. Confirm important assumptions with real users before agreeing a final scope or fixed price. Use anonymised examples and ask permission before viewing personal data.

## Suggested 60-minute agenda

| Time | Topic |
| --- | --- |
| 0–10 minutes | Problem and user |
| 10–25 minutes | Current workflow |
| 25–40 minutes | First release |
| 40–50 minutes | Constraints and risk |
| 50–60 minutes | Budget and next steps |

## Problem and user

- Who will use this first? What is their role?
- What are they trying to finish, and when does the problem occur?
- What happened the last time they tried to do it?
- What happens if nothing changes for another three months?
- Who buys or approves the product, and who uses it daily?

## Current workflow

- What starts the task and what information is needed?
- Which register, spreadsheet or app is used now?
- Where does someone wait, copy information or check it by hand?
- How often does the task happen, and what does a mistake cost?
- What happens when the internet or power is unavailable?

## First release

- What must the first user do from start to finish?
- What result tells them the task is complete?
- What can the team do manually for a small pilot?
- Which requests can wait without blocking that task?
- What would make this release unsafe or unusable?

## Constraints and risk

- What data will the product hold, and who may see or change it?
- Do we need payments, messaging or another system for the first workflow?
- Who owns those accounts, and can we test access before committing?
- What must work on a phone, in Urdu or during a connection failure?
- What happens if someone makes a mistake or data needs restoring?

## Budget and next steps

- What budget range has been set aside for the first release?
- Is there a real deadline, and what happens if it moves?
- Who approves the scope and who signs off the work?
- Who supplies content, records, accounts and feedback?
- How will hosting, third-party charges and ongoing support be paid for?
