# Practical Ideal Customer Profile Worksheet

Companion worksheet for Part 4 of the “Distribution Before Product-Market Fit” series.

An early ICP should not be a polished description of an imaginary perfect customer. Use this worksheet to build a testable account hypothesis, qualify real prospects, document evidence, and revise the profile after every ten qualified customer conversations.

## How to use this worksheet

Complete it in four passes:

1. Draft the hypothesis using current knowledge.
2. Research real accounts and score at least 30 of them.
3. Validate the profile through customer conversations and commercial behavior.
4. Revise the ICP after every ten qualified conversations.

Use these evidence labels throughout:

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| A: Assumption | A reasonable belief without direct customer evidence |
| S: Customer statement | Something a customer or prospect told you |
| O: Observed behavior | Something you witnessed in the customer’s workflow |
| C: Commercial evidence | Payment, pilot commitment, internal introduction, data access, renewal, or another costly action |

Do not treat A or S as equivalent to O or C.

### Problem evidence level

Select the highest verified level:

- Level 0: Assumed. We believe the problem exists.
- Level 1: Acknowledged. The prospect agrees that it exists.
- Level 2: Experienced. The prospect can describe a recent occurrence.
- Level 3: Measured. The organization can estimate its cost or impact.
- Level 4: Prioritized. Responsibility, budget, or a deadline has been assigned.
- Level 5: Active. The organization is currently evaluating or implementing a solution.

### Alternative-satisfaction test

- What does the current process do well?
- Why has the organization kept using it?
- What would be lost by replacing it?

Possible trigger categories

- Operational: upcoming high-volume cycle; expansion into another location; significant increase in customers or users; key employee resignation; recent operational failure; backlog or processing delay; introduction of a new workflow.

The worksheet is ready to use when you have:

Do not wait for every field to become certain. Complete the unknown fields honestly, use the worksheet to select real accounts, and let observed behavior and commercial commitments improve the next version.
