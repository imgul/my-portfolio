# Feature decision record

## Feature
What capability is being considered?

## Customer problem
What customer outcome or pain does it address?

## Evidence and confidence
What interviews, observed workarounds, usage patterns, support requests, sales losses or paid commitments support the need? What is still uncertain? Is the sample representative?

## Options considered
- Build:
- Buy:
- Integrate:
- Ignore or defer:

## Estimated costs
Record assumptions and ranges for initial implementation, recurring vendor and infrastructure costs, maintenance, support, security, migration or exit, and opportunity cost. Compare over a stated time horizon.

## Strategic assessment
Does the capability create differentiation, necessary control, proprietary learning or a data advantage? Which components are unique and which are commodities?

## Risks and constraints
Consider security, privacy, legal obligations, accessibility, reliability, adoption, vendor dependency and operational ownership. Identify any hard requirements that a score cannot override.

## Decision
State the selected option and why it is better than the alternatives. Name the owner and what work will be deferred to make room.

## Review trigger
Record the date, measurable change or new evidence that would cause the team to reconsider the decision.
